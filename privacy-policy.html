Privacy Policy NotesPix.

Learn what information NotesPix processes and how it is used to provide the student resource platform.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This page explains the intended privacy practices for the current NotesPix website. Review it against your actual hosting, analytics, and data-retention setup before public launch.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how NotesPix handles information when you use the website and its student-resource features. It should be read with our Terms & Conditions.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: name, email address, account identifier, and authentication-related information managed through Supabase.
  • Signup declarations: the account may store a record that you confirmed the age/parental-permission statement and the date/time you accepted the Terms. NotesPix does not ask for your date of birth in the signup form.
  • Content: resource titles, descriptions, subject and format details, and files you choose to upload.
  • Usage information: actions such as publishing, saving, or reporting resources, where these features are enabled.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, and diagnostic information that may be processed by the website or its hosting and service providers.

3. How information is used

We use information to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, store and display resources, provide saved-resource and activity features, respond to reports and support requests, protect the service, and maintain or improve NotesPix.

4. Storage and service providers

NotesPix uses Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. Information may be processed on infrastructure operated by Supabase and its hosting providers. Their handling of information is also subject to their applicable terms and privacy documentation. We do not publish passwords in the NotesPix database; authentication is handled by the authentication provider.

5. Resource visibility

Resources you publish may be available to other NotesPix visitors, and files stored in a public resource bucket may be accessible through their file links. Do not upload sensitive, confidential, or personal information. Only upload content you are authorised to share.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate NotesPix, disclosed when required by law, or used where reasonably necessary to protect users, enforce our terms, investigate abuse, or secure the service.

7. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the platform. We use access controls and available security features, but no online service or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You can keep your account credentials secure and avoid uploading information you do not want shared. For requests to access, correct, or delete account-related information, contact the NotesPix team through the website. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

9. Children and younger users

NotesPix is intended for students and learners. If you are below the age at which you can independently consent to online services under applicable law, use the service with parent or legal-guardian involvement and permission.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as NotesPix evolves. Changes will be posted here with a revised date. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact method provided on the NotesPix website.
