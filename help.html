HELP & SUPPORT

How can we help you?

Find answers about your NotesPix account, shared resources, uploads, and saved notes.

Frequently asked questions

Choose a question to see the answer.

How do I find study resources?

Open Explore Resources to browse published resources. Use the search box and available filters to find materials by title, subject, or format. Open a resource to view its details.

How do I save a resource?

Open the resource details page and select Save. You can find your saved items later in Saved Notes. You need to be signed in to save resources.

How do I upload and publish a resource?

Sign in, open Upload, complete the required resource details, choose an allowed document, and submit it. NotesPix stores the resource record in its database and the file in NotesPix Storage. Published resources can be discovered by other students.

Which file types and sizes are supported?

The current NotesPix upload setup accepts PDF, DOC/DOCX, PPT/PPTX, and XLS/XLSX files, with a maximum file size of 10 MB per upload. Upload availability can also depend on the storage quota of the NotesPix project.

Who can see the resources I publish?

Resources marked as published are visible in Explore Resources. Do not upload private documents, personal records, copyrighted material you do not have permission to share, or files containing sensitive information.

Where can I manage my account?

Open My Profile to view your account information and account actions. Use Settings to change your appearance and dashboard display preferences.

How do I change my password?

Open your profile or account settings and use the password-change option. If you cannot access your account, use the password recovery option on the login page, if available.

How do I report a resource or a problem?

Use the report option on the relevant resource page to report a resource. For account, upload, or website problems, send a support request using the form on this page. Include the page name and what you expected to happen.
